El usuario pide una sola prueba: Poro golpeando, formas y contornos sencillos de cartas de Kio, colores apagados de La Leyenda y poca magia. Esta aclaracion sustituye las propuestas anteriores de tres variantes y los motivos naturales sin Poro.

## Prompt
Create ONE horizontal ATTACK CARD ILLUSTRATION only, target aspect ratio exactly 25:19, to be resized to 250x190 pixels. No card frame, text, lettering, numbers or UI. References 1 and 2 are STYLE references: match the existing Kio card art with clean bold outlines, simple broad shapes, flat cel shading with only 2-3 shade levels, tightly framed readable action pose, sparse graphic background. Reference 3 is ONLY Poro character identity and palette: a round fluffy honey-beige and cream woodland creature, small rounded ears, short front paws, dark eyes and a very large round BROWN nose. Do not copy the watercolor texture or detailed forest of reference 3. Poro is NOT a giraffe or dragon, has no horns, no clothes. Scene: tight close-up of Poro delivering a forceful ordinary punch with one short front paw toward the right. His face and upper torso fill left-center, determined brow, brown nose clearly visible, punching fist foreshortened but recognizable as a soft furry paw. A SMALL simple muted ivory impact shape on the right and 2 or 3 subtle motion lines communicate a normal physical hit. No luminous claws, no spell, no glowing magic, no halos, no moon, no particles. Muted low-saturation book colors only: cream #E5D9C0, sandy honey-beige #BDA078, moss-brown #6C6555, slate grey-blue #75818A, dark brown outlines #403A33. Clear contrast but absolutely no saturated orange, cyan, yellow or red. Simple slate-grey-blue graphic background with just a few abstract angular shapes, no detailed landscape. Cute but serious serene guardian, not silly. Flat cartoon illustration, NOT watercolor, NOT realistic fur, NOT painterly texture. Symbolic physical basic punch, strong visual clarity at 250x190.

## Edicion de Defender
Modo integrado image_gen: reducir saturacion aproximadamente 30 %, preservar pose, anatomia, composicion, arco defensivo y trazos de la imagen adjunta; conservar color sin convertir a gris. Recorte central 25:19 y reduccion bicubica a 250x190.
